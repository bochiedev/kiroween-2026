PaperFault marker cards

Print instructions

  1. Print this page at 100% scale (no "fit to page"), on white paper, in black ink.
  2. Cut out each card. Keep the white border around the black square.
  3. Use matte paper. Glossy paper causes glare that hides the marker.
  4. Hold each card flat and well lit, facing the camera, about 20 to 40 cm away.
  5. Removing a card from the camera view takes that component down. Bringing it back restores it.
  6. Physical mode is implemented but not hardware-verified.

Open physical mode or use the Digital Lab (no camera).
